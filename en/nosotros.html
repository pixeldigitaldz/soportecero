About SoporteCero

SoporteCero is an independent technical documentation platform dedicated to providing direct, tested, and zero-friction solutions for system administrators, web developers, and Linux gaming enthusiasts.

🚀 Our Mission

In modern technology, spending hours diagnosing cryptic terminal errors, Docker permission failures, or game performance stutters under Proton is a common headache. Our mission is to reduce downtime to zero by offering clear diagnostics, copy-paste verified command blocks, and practical prevention tips.

💻 Areas of Expertise

  • Systems & Servers: Configuration and troubleshooting for Nginx, Linux (Arch, Ubuntu, Debian), Docker, Umbrel OS, LVM, SSH, and sysadmin networking.
  • Gaming Tech: Linux gaming performance tweaks, Proton/VKD3D/DXVK compatibility, Vulkan shaders, OptiScaler, and tools like MangoHud and GameMode.
  • Web & Code: Troubleshooting Node.js, Express, React, Next.js, WordPress, Cloudflare SSL, CORS policies, and Git workflows.

🛡️ Commitment to Quality

All our tutorials feature a Quick Diagnostics section with a comparative table of symptoms and root causes, exact command snippets ready to copy, and security recommendations to keep errors from recurring.
